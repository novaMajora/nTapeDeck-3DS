# Changelog

## 0.2.0

First release.

- Six themes: Tape Deck (default), Wave, Click Wheel, Portable, Media Blue and Dashboard.
  Switch from the palette button on the bottom screen or with SELECT; the last theme is
  remembered.
- Library built from ID3 tags: All Songs, Artists, Albums, Genres and Folders, with album art
  from embedded covers or `cover.jpg`/`folder.jpg`.
- Playlists: `.m3u` files, Favorites (the heart button) and Recently Added.
- Shuffle, repeat (all / one), seeking, volume and a spectrum visualizer.
- Music keeps playing with the lid closed (screens off).
- Installable `.cia` with a Home Menu banner, plus a `.3dsx` for the Homebrew Launcher.
